The jump type object

Type: String

Allowed values:

Value Description
"dc" Da Capo ("D.C."). Performance continues from the beginning of the piece.
"dcalcoda" Da Capo al Coda ("D.C. al Coda"). Performance continues from the beginning of the piece until the "tocoda" jump, then continues from the coda sign.
"dcalfine" Da Capo al Fine ("D.C. al Fine"). Performance continues from the beginning of the piece and ends at the fine.
"dsalcoda" Dal Segno al Coda ("D.S. al Coda"). Performance continues from the segno until the "tocoda" jump, then continues from the coda sign.
"dsalfine" Dal Segno al Fine ("D.S. al Fine"). Performance continues from the segno and ends at the fine.
"segno" Dal Segno ("D.S."). Performance continues from the segno.
"tocoda" To Coda. Performance continues from the coda sign.

This jump is ignored until a "dcalcoda" or "dsalcoda" jump has been taken.
